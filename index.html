What is
AD-Meals?

AD-MEALS is a digital platform designed to simplify the school canteen experience. It allows students to explore available menus, place orders in advance, and collect their meals when they are ready, helping reduce waiting times during school breaks.

Our Research Results

Students often face long queues when buying food and drinks during school breaks. With five canteens and limited staff, waiting times can become longer. AD-MEALS provides a faster and easier way to order and pick up meals.

Frequency of Long Queues

The data shows that long queues are a recurring issue, with 60% of respondents experiencing them sometimes, 14% often, and 6% very often.

Impact on Purchase Decisions

The data shows that most respondents have experienced skipping a purchase due to long queues.

Demand for Pre-order Feature

76% of respondents find the pre-order feature useful, indicating strong demand for this solution.
