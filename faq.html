Frequently
Asked Questions

What is AD-MEALS?

AD-MEALS is a digital platform that makes ordering food from the school canteen faster and more convenient.

How do i place an order?

Choose a canteen, select your food, add it to your cart, choose a pickup time, and complete your order.

Can i see my previous orders?

Yes, you can. Your previous orders can be viewed in the Order History section.

How can i know when my order is ready?

You can track your order status through AD-MEALS and receive a notification when it is ready.
